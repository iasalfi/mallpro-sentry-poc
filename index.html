What this camera is doing

It sits overhead at the doorway — that's the placement that actually holds up accuracy-wise. The amber line is the counting line. Every tracked person gets a box and an ID, and the instant that box crosses the line, direction and all, it's logged. Someone hesitates at the door and steps back? Doesn't get counted twice.

This is body detection, not face recognition — abaya, hijab, niqab, none of it changes the count. Every crossing gets counted the same way. Anything face-dependent lives one tab over, in Visitors.
Module A+ · Visitor insight (optional)

Visitors & demographics

Every crossing gets a gender and age-band estimate — a face read where there's a face to read, body cues where there isn't. It's aggregate only. Nobody's identity gets stored or matched to them.

Face signatures: memory-only, auto-expire
Face visibility in a KSA mall

Hijab covers hair, not face. SCRFD still finds it, InsightFace still reads it — no change in estimation at all. Niqab or burqa is the actual variable. The face is gone, but the covering itself tells you something: MallPro still calls the visit female, then falls back to height, build and gait for a rougher age band. Every one of those reads gets tagged body-cue with a lower confidence score attached — it's never dressed up as a face match. Try the control below and watch the mix shift at a more conservative site.
