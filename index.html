Tenants

Tenant footfall

People entering and leaving each tenant shop, counted at the entrance or shopfront with a two-line gate. Staff are left out, and shoppers leaving with the tenant's bag are counted as bag-outs.

--:--:--
How counting works
Two-line gate

Every entrance has two count lines, A then B. A person counts as in after crossing A and then B, and as out after B and then A. Someone who walks up to the line and turns back is not counted twice.

Staff left out

Mall and souq staff are recognised by uniform and kept out of the shopper counts. They appear in the excluded staff tally for each tenant.

Bags counted

Shoppers leaving with the tenant's branded bag are counted as bag-outs, shown as a share of exits. Bag-outs drive the revenue estimate.

Tenants

Revenue estimate

What each tenant could be earning: bag-outs multiplied by an average basket value you agree with the tenant. Edit the basket value and the figures change straight away.

This is an estimate built from bag-outs and an agreed basket value. It is not audited sales. Enter what a tenant reports to see how far the estimate sits from it.
